## Add a claim when its evidence becomes available

Record the claim ID, exact wording, run IDs, code/config hashes, hardware, inputs, metric definition, all attempts, exclusions and limitations. Link the raw source and the script that derives the reported value. If evidence contradicts a claim, revise the claim and affected drafts.

A successful engineering checkpoint supports its recorded test scope. It does not establish novelty or broad superiority. Tests, frames, missions and independent repetitions must be counted separately.
